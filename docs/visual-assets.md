## Final neural image prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: atmospheric photographic backdrop for a high-end interactive creative portfolio website, landscape 16:9, no interface or text. Create an ultra-detailed cinematic macro view through a living neural network, futuristic movie visual effects rendered with physical photographic realism. Black graphite environment, silver membrane textures, deep midnight blue and faint teal scattering, icy blue white electrical energy with very restrained violet. Fine organic dendrites and axons, naturally irregular softly translucent neuron cell bodies with tiny metallic-looking refractive microtextures, bright delicate nuclei; some sharply in focus, others dissolving into true lens depth of field and atmospheric bokeh. Neural electrical filaments, no polygonal meshes, no wireframe spheres. Composition is essential: left 45% of the landscape must stay almost empty near-black with only a trace of mist for website text; richly dimensional interwoven neural tissue occupies the right 55%, THREE larger tactile glowing neuron bodies as focal points between 60-90% image width and 30-70% height. Dense hair-fine branching fibres recede in depth, elegant photographic highlights with restrained bloom, dynamic but calm visual composition, organic asymmetric interconnected labyrinth of thought. Frame with peripheral dark foreground fibres, make it feel like a very high budget science-fiction film macro shot. The image should read as physically photographed, not schematic clipart, not faceted, not a network diagram. No text, letters, numbers, logos, watermarks, branding, UI, brain outline, or flat line illustration. Keep shadows dark, details excellent and the right-hand glow controlled to protect the left-hand reading space.

The AI portfolio photographs are the user's supplied files from Drive, not new demonstration images generated for this build. Their source names and web asset mapping are in `docs/ai-assets.json`.
